            Each listener moves their slider from 0 (bad) to 5 (excellent) for every clip they heard. A slider counts once it is moved. The mean over listeners is the clip's MOS.

                Listen to the voice's original recording first. Then score every clip from 0 (bad) to 5 (excellent) in steps of 0.5, for how good and expressive it sounds as speech, and tick the boxes if you hear an artifact or a different person than the original. The clips come in a mixed order and are all scored the same way. A score is saved as soon as you set it, and you can change it at any time.
