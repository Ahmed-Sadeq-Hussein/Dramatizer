VOICE LIBRARY
=============

The speech datasets Dramatizer 2 uses as example voices, and where to get them.

Every dataset here allows both academic and commercial use. The two that are
downloaded share no speakers: each was recorded by a different group of people.

Local copy (this machine): D:/Voices library/<folder>
Downloaded: 26 October 2026


DOWNLOADED
==========

1. VCTK (version 0.92)
----------------------
Folder:     D:/Voices library/VCTK
Licence:    CC BY 4.0 (free for any use; credit the dataset)
Speakers:   109 English speakers with many accents (English, Scottish, Irish,
            American, Canadian, Indian, South African, ...). VCTK has 110,
            but speaker p315 has no transcripts and is not in this copy
Clips:      88,156 (44,078 sentences, each from two microphones)
Speech:     Every speaker reads the same sentences, studio quality, two
            microphones per sentence
Size:       about 18 GB (parquet files)
Download:   https://huggingface.co/datasets/jspaulsen/vctk
            (a copy with leading and trailing silence trimmed)
Original:   https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443
            (University of Edinburgh DataShare, not GitHub or Hugging Face)

2. CREMA-D
----------
Folder:     D:/Voices library/CREMA-D
Licence:    Open Database License (ODbL): free for any use; credit the
            dataset, and share changed versions of the dataset itself under
            the same licence
Speakers:   91 actors (48 male, 43 female), ages 20 to 74, varied ethnicities
Speech:     12 sentences acted in 6 emotions (anger, disgust, fear, happy,
            neutral, sad) at several intensities. 7,442 clips, 16 kHz
Size:       about 0.5 GB; the clips are unpacked in CREMA-D/audio
Download:   https://huggingface.co/datasets/myleslinder/crema-d
Original:   https://github.com/CheyneyComputerScience/CREMA-D


NOT DOWNLOADED (also free for academic and commercial use)
==========================================================

GLOBE (version 2)      CC0. 23,519 speakers with worldwide English accents,
                       about 76 GB.
                       https://huggingface.co/datasets/MushanW/GLOBE_V2
LibriTTS-R             CC BY 4.0. 2,456 audiobook readers, about 101 GB.
                       https://huggingface.co/datasets/mythicinfinity/libritts_r
Mozilla Common Voice   CC0. Tens of thousands of speakers, 100+ languages.
                       No longer on Hugging Face: download it from
                       https://datacollective.mozillafoundation.org
                       Shares speakers with GLOBE, which was built from it.
LibriVox               Public domain audiobooks. Not a dataset on GitHub or
                       Hugging Face: https://librivox.org
                       Shares speakers with LibriTTS-R, which was built from it.
HiFi-TTS               CC BY 4.0. 10 LibriVox readers, so they may also be in
                       LibriTTS-R. https://huggingface.co/datasets/MikhailT/hifi-tts

Take at most one from each overlapping pair (GLOBE or Common Voice; LibriTTS-R,
LibriVox or HiFi-TTS) to keep every voice in one dataset only.

A NOTE ON USING THESE VOICES
============================

The licences cover the recordings. The speakers agreed to speech research
and speech technology, not to having a product imitate them personally.
Use these voices to develop and test; clone only voices whose owners have
given explicit consent for anything you publish or sell.
