DATA LISTING
============

The data this project uses: how big each part is, and where it came from.
Sizes were measured on disk on 5 November 2026 (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes).
Local copy: D:/Voices library/<folder>. The audio itself is not in this repository.

Details of each dataset: "Voice library.txt".
Exactly which clips are used, with a checksum for each: data/manifests/voices-v1.json.
